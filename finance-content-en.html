SECTION 1

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report presents the revenue, expenditure and closing financial position of the American Ditek Institute (ADI), Yaoundé, for the 2025/2026 academic year. It is compiled from the Finance Office’s end-of-year records and is addressed to the Super Administrator. All amounts are expressed in Central African CFA francs (FCFA).

Key figureAmount (FCFA)
Total revenue, 2025/20264,528,000
Total expenditure, 2025/20264,509,686
Surplus for the year18,314
Amount owed by students at year end1,691,000

Principal findings

  • Revenue of FCFA 4,528,000 was derived from tuition (FCFA 4,408,000; 97.35%) and registration income (FCFA 120,000; 2.65%).
  • Expenditure of FCFA 4,509,686 absorbed 99.60% of revenue, leaving a surplus of FCFA 18,314, or 0.40% of revenue.
  • Lecturers’ first-semester teaching hours (36.75%) and other operating expenditures (30.09%) together represent two-thirds of all expenditure.
  • Several costs remain unpaid for lack of funds. At the rates recorded in this report, the indicative unpaid obligations (FCFA 3,303,700, excluding the water bill) exceed the FCFA 1,691,000 owed by students; the surplus therefore does not represent a net cash reserve.
  • Four arithmetic differences found in the handwritten records were reconciled (Section 6). The UB inspection total was corrected to the sum of its lines, which reduces expenditure by FCFA 2,700; the accounts balance at the figures shown.

2.2. Analysis of expenditure

SECTION 7

BASIS OF PREPARATION AND AUTHORISATION

7.1. Basis of preparation

  • This report was prepared from the Finance Office’s end-of-year financial records for the 2025/2026 academic year. The amounts are as recorded and have not been audited.
  • The tuition breakdown by programme and level was not part of the material used; tuition revenue is taken from the recorded grand total.
  • Some handwriting was unclear, notably student names and item descriptions. Spellings should be confirmed.
  • The abbreviation “UB” is kept as written in the Finance Office’s record.
  • Percentages are rounded to two decimal places. Amounts in Section 5 are indicative, as explained there.
